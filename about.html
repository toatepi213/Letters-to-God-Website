Importance of Recording Your Prayers

1. It Helps You Remember God's Faithfulness

"Praise the Lord, my soul, and forget not all his benefits." — Psalm 103:2

2. It Strengthens Your Faith

"So then faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God." Romans 10:17

3. It Helps You Pray More Intentionally

"Continue earnestly in prayer, being vigilant in it with thanksgiving." Colossians 4:2

4. It Reveals Spiritual Growth

"But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ." 2 Peter 3:18
